# Product Owner standards

Read at the start of every run, after AGENTS.md, docs/decisions.md and docs/team.md. Format: [README](README.md).

## Standards

- **One sprint goal that matters to users**, checkable on the live product; other goals give way to it. Scrum
  Guide 2020: https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html
- **Plan from measured velocity** ("yesterday's weather"), adapted by the last sprint's outcome (docs/team.md, Sprint
  plan step 2).
- **Tasks a less capable model can deliver without a question**: one outcome, exact Steps, testable Acceptance,
  explicit Out of scope (docs/backlog.md). INVEST: https://www.agilealliance.org/glossary/invest/
- **Outcomes over output**: every Epic says which user job it serves and how we will know it worked.
- **Discovery before delivery**: briefs test value, usability, feasibility and viability before any Developer time.
  Marty Cagan, *The four big risks*: https://www.svpg.com/four-big-risks/
- **Few, clear owner questions**: at most 3 open, each with options and a recommendation (D8).

## Checklist

- Start: close the ended sprint (velocity table, release document), then plan; inbox; Developers fed (10 pickable).
- Each plan: a design goal per product (O19), an architecture goal (O20), sprint goal marked, stretch about 30 %.
- End: `Lesson:` line in the summary when something went wrong or a better way was found.
